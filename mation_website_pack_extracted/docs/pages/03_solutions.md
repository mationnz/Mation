### 2) Role-based interactive tabs (recommended)

Tabs: Executives / Ops & PMs / Compliance / Field / IT & Security

Each tab follows the same template:

**Pain → What Mation generates → Outcomes → Example workflows**

---

#### Executives

**Pain:** Visibility is fragmented; decisions lag; risk shows up late.  
**Generates:** evidence-backed summaries, cross-system dashboards, risk and action views.  
**Outcomes:** faster decisions, fewer surprises, better accountability.  
**Example workflows:** “Weekly exec pack,” “Risk hotspots,” “Portfolio status.”

---

#### Ops & PMs

**Pain:** Hand-offs, reporting, and coordination consume time.  
**Generates:** project dashboards, action lists, minutes, follow-ups, reporting artifacts.  
**Outcomes:** less admin, clearer next steps, fewer missed items.  
**Example workflows:** “Daily update rollup,” “Meeting → actions,” “Status to client.”

---

#### Compliance & quality

**Pain:** Evidence volume exceeds human bandwidth; audit prep is reactive.  
**Generates:** evidence bundles, compliance packs, overdue checklists, audit trails.  
**Outcomes:** audit readiness, proactive risk control.  
**Example workflows:** “Audit pack generator,” “Training completion tracking,” “Exception alerts.”

---

#### Field / workforce

**Pain:** Daily reporting is a chore; poor data quality flows upward.  
**Generates:** fast capture flows (diaries, hazards, incidents), photo-based evidence, structured outputs.  
**Outcomes:** better data, faster follow-up, fewer disputes.  
**Example workflows:** “Daily diary capture,” “Hazard report,” “Incident evidence pack.”

---

#### IT & Security

**Pain:** AI fails when it’s uncontrolled, unlogged, and ungoverned.  
**Generates:** tool gateway controls, RBAC, approval gates, audit trails, observability.  
**Outcomes:** deployable AI in production, with compliance posture intact.  
**Example workflows:** “Read-only rollout,” “Approval-gated actions,” “Connector governance.”
