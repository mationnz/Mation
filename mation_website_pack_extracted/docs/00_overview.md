# 00 — Overview

Mation is positioned as an **AI operating system for complex work**.

The website must achieve two things at the same time:

1) **Win the operator/executive**: show immediate business value (speed, clarity, fewer handoffs, repeatable outputs).
2) **Win the technical gatekeeper**: prove this is deployable (governance, audit trails, modular architecture, safe execution).

This pack treats the website as a **product experience**, not a brochure:
- the hero section demonstrates the generative interface with a scripted interactive widget
- the architecture and security pages contain the details needed for a Head of Technology to say “yes”
- the services page makes it clear Mation delivers outcomes, not just software access

---

## The narrative (the thread that ties every page together)

**Ask → Assemble → Act (with receipts)**

- Ask: users speak naturally (chat/voice)
- Assemble: Mation generates a cockpit UI (tables, charts, forms, artifacts, buttons)
- Act: Mation executes workflows safely (permissions, approvals, audit trails)

---

## What not to do (common AI website mistakes)

- Avoid generic “AI magic” claims without showing the product.
- Avoid “we replace everything” language. We connect and orchestrate.
- Avoid robot imagery and sci‑fi stock visuals.
- Avoid making users think they need prompt engineering skills.

---

## Primary CTA strategy

- Primary: **Book a demo**
- Secondary: **Talk to an architect**
- Tertiary: **See the interface** (interactive demo)
