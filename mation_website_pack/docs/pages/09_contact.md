# Contact / Book a demo page

## SEO

- **Meta title:** Book a demo — Mation

- **Meta description:** Request a demo of Mation. We’ll propose a pilot scope and security-first rollout plan tailored to your systems and workflows.


---

## Page goal

Convert with a clean demo request experience and qualify leads.


---

## Layout + copy


### 1) Hero

**H1:** Book a demo  
**Sub:** Tell us what systems you use and what workflows you want to improve. We’ll propose a pilot scope and rollout plan.

---

### 2) Form fields

Required:
- Name
- Work email
- Company
- Role
- Industry (dropdown)
- Systems in use (multi-select)
- What do you want to automate or improve? (textarea)

Optional:
- Timeline (0–30 days, 30–90, 90+)
- Compliance constraints (checkboxes: SOC2, ISO, HIPAA etc — generic)
- Notes

CTA button: Request demo

Microcopy below CTA:
“We respond with a proposed pilot scope and a security-first rollout plan.”

---

### 3) Post-submit success state

Title: “Thanks — we’ll be in touch.”  
Copy: “Expect a reply with pilot options, security posture questions, and next steps.”

---

### 4) Alternative CTA

If user is technical:
Link: “Talk to an architect”
